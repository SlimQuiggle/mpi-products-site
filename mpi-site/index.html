About Us

About MPI Products

Founded on the radical belief that metal should be flat — like, obsessively, concerningly flat — MPI Products has spent decades perfecting the art of fineblanking. While other companies were out there living their lives, we were in the shop, measuring flatness to the sixth decimal place and arguing about microns. We hold IATF and ISO 14001 certifications, which is corporate-speak for 'we care about quality so much it's basically a personality disorder.'

From 150-ton presses to 1,500-ton presses, we don't just stamp metal — we give it an existential crisis about what it means to be truly flat. Steel, aluminum, copper, bi-metal — we don't discriminate. If it's metal and it needs to be flat, we're already emotionally invested.

What We Do

Our Expertise

Four ways we make metal do things it didn't know it could do.

⚙️

Precision Fineblanking

Our bread and butter. We take sheets of metal and blank them so fine that electron microscopes send us fan mail. Tolerances so tight they make Swiss watchmakers nervous. So flat, physicists ask us for calibration references.

🔧

Progressive & Transfer Stamping

Multiple operations, one continuous flow, zero drama. Our progressive dies move metal through stages like a spa treatment — except instead of leaving relaxed, it leaves dimensionally perfect. We hit metal repeatedly and call it precision.

🏗️

Precision Grinding

For when fineblanking alone isn't enough to satisfy our deeply personal need for perfection. We grind surfaces to tolerances that would make a laser level feel inadequate. We grind because we care. Possibly too much.

📐

CNC Machining

Computer-controlled cutting for complex geometries that make normal machinists quietly excuse themselves from the room. If you can CAD it, we can machine it. If you can't CAD it, we'll judge you silently. Letting robots do the precision work since before it was cool.
